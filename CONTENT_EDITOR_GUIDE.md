## 1. Welcome — What is Sanity Studio?

**Sanity Studio** is your content dashboard. Think of it as the control panel for the Beacon of Blessings website. Everything that appears on the website — the project pages, blog posts, gallery photos, founder bios, statistics, and contact information — can be changed from Sanity Studio without touching a single line of code.

You log into Sanity Studio in your web browser, make your changes, click **Publish**, and the website updates automatically. That is all there is to it.

Here is what Sanity Studio manages for the website:

| What you see on the website                      | What it is called in Sanity Studio |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Projects page (e.g., School Supplies Drive 2024) | **Project**                        |
| Blog posts                                       | **Blog Post**                      |
| Gallery photos                                   | **Gallery Item**                   |
| Resources / Documents (PDFs, reports)            | Managed separately (see Section 7) |
| Founder profiles on the About page               | **Founder**                        |
| Core Values on the About page                    | **Core Value**                     |
| Impact numbers (e.g., "500+ Students Supported") | **Impact Statistic**               |
| Organization name, email, phone, address         | **Site Configuration**             |

## 3. How Content Updates Work

### The simple version

1. You make a change in Sanity Studio.
2. You click the green **Publish** button.
3. The website updates automatically — no extra steps needed.

### How long does it take?

After you click Publish, changes appear on the live website within:

- **60 seconds** — for most content (Projects, Blog Posts, Gallery photos)
- **5 minutes (300 seconds)** — for less frequently changed content (Founder profiles, Core Values, Impact Statistics, Site Configuration)

If your change is not showing yet, simply wait the appropriate time and then refresh the page.

### Drafts vs. Published

Sanity Studio saves your work in two states:

- **Draft** — changes you have made but not yet published. Only you can see these; they do not appear on the website yet. Drafts are saved automatically as you type, so you will never lose your work.
- **Published** — the live version that visitors see on the website.

The green **Publish** button at the top right of the editing screen moves your draft to the published state. Until you click it, nothing changes on the website.

> **Important:** Always click Publish when you want your changes to go live. If you close the browser without publishing, your draft is still saved and you can publish it later.

## 4. Managing Projects

Projects are the programs and initiatives Beacon of Blessings runs — for example, the School Supplies Drive 2024 or the upcoming Digital Learning Initiative. Each project has its own page on the website.

### 4.1 — View existing projects

1. In the left sidebar of Sanity Studio, click **Project**.
2. You will see a list of all existing projects.
3. Click on any project title to open and read it.

### 4.2 — Create a new project

1. In the left sidebar, click **Project**.
2. Click the **pencil icon** or **Create new** button (usually at the top right of the list).
3. A blank project form opens. Fill in each field as described below.

### 4.3 — Fill in each field

Here is what every field means and what to put in it:

---

**📌 Title** _(required)_
The name of the project as it will appear on the website.

Example: `School Supplies Drive 2025`

---

**🔗 Slug** _(required)_
This is the "URL-friendly" name for the project — the part that appears in the web address.

Example: If your project is called "School Supplies Drive 2025", the slug would be `school-supplies-drive-2025`.

**You do not need to type this yourself.** After you fill in the Title, click the **Generate** button next to the Slug field. Sanity will create the slug automatically from the title.

> **Warning:** Once a project is published and people can visit its page, do not change the slug. Changing it will break any links people have saved to that page.

---

**📋 Status** _(required)_
Choose one of two options from the dropdown menu:

- **Completed** — the project has already happened
- **Upcoming** — the project is planned for the future

Example: School Supplies Drive 2024 is **Completed**. Digital Learning Initiative is **Upcoming**.

---

**📅 Date**
The time period when the project took place or will take place. This is free-form text, so write it in whatever way makes sense.

Examples:

- `June - September 2024`
- `2026`
- `January 2025`

---

**💰 Budget**
The project budget in Nigerian Naira.

Example: `N2.5M`

---

**📝 Description**
A paragraph or two describing what the project is, what it does, and who it helps.

Example:

> Our inaugural project distributed essential school supplies to 500+ students across 5 communities in Lagos. Items included school bags, notebooks, textbooks, and writing materials.

---

**✅ Impact Points**
A list of specific results or goals for the project. Each one is a short sentence. These appear as bullet points on the project page.

To add an impact point:

1. Click **Add item**.
2. Type your sentence.
3. Click **Add item** again to add more.

Examples:

- `500+ students received school supplies`
- `5 Lagos communities served`
- `2,000+ notebooks distributed`

---

**⭐ Featured**
A toggle (on/off switch) that determines whether this project appears prominently on the homepage.

- Switch it **on** (toggle turns blue or green) to feature the project on the homepage.
- Leave it **off** to keep it visible only on the Projects page.

Tip: Feature only your 2–3 most important projects. Too many featured projects makes the homepage feel cluttered.

---

**🖼️ Image**
A photo that represents this project. See Section 4.6 below for how to upload an image.

---

### 4.4 — Mark a project as Completed vs. Upcoming

In the **Status** dropdown, select:

- **Completed** if the project has finished.
- **Upcoming** if it is still being planned or fundraised for.

Completed projects appear in one section on the Projects page; upcoming projects appear in another. The website handles the sorting automatically.

### 4.5 — Feature a project on the homepage

Find the **Featured** field. It looks like a toggle switch or a checkbox. Turn it **on** to feature the project. The project will then appear in the "Our Projects" section on the homepage.

To un-feature a project (remove it from the homepage but keep it on the Projects page), turn the Featured toggle **off**.

### 4.6 — Upload a project image

1. Find the **Image** field at the bottom of the project form.
2. Click **Upload**.
3. A file picker opens. Navigate to the photo on your computer and select it.
4. The photo will upload and appear as a thumbnail. You may see a small crop/hotspot tool — this lets you choose which part of the photo stays in view when the website crops it. Drag the circle to the most important part of the photo (usually a person's face or the main subject).
5. The image is now attached to the project.

**Image tips:**

- Minimum recommended size: 1,200 pixels wide
- Accepted formats: JPEG (.jpg) or PNG (.png)
- Maximum file size: 5 MB. If your photo is larger, resize it before uploading (see Section 12 for how).

### 4.7 — Publish the project

When all fields are filled in, look at the top right corner of the screen. You will see a green button that says **Publish**. Click it.

The project is now live on the website. Within 60 seconds, it will appear on the Projects page (and the homepage if you set Featured to on).

## 5. Writing Blog Posts

Blog posts are articles that appear in the Blog section of the website. Examples include impact stories, ministry updates, year-end reports, and community news.

### 5.1 — Create a new blog post

1. In the left sidebar, click **Blog Post**.
2. Click **Create new**.
3. Fill in each field as described below.

### 5.2 — Fill in each field

---

**📌 Title** _(required)_
The headline of the blog post.

Example: `Transforming Lives Through Education`

---

**🔗 Slug** _(required)_
The URL-friendly name for the post. After you fill in the Title, click **Generate** next to the Slug field. Do not change the slug after publishing.

---

**📋 Excerpt**
A short 1–3 sentence summary of the post. This appears on the blog listing page below the title, so readers know what the post is about before clicking.

Example:

> See how our School Supplies Drive 2024 is making a lasting impact on students across Lagos communities.

---

**📝 Content**
The full text of the blog post. Write everything you want to say here. Press Enter to start new paragraphs.

---

**👤 Author**
The name of the person who wrote the post.

Examples: `Grace Kure`, `Lionel Tchami`, `Beacon of Blessings Team`

---

**📅 Date**
The date the post was written or published. Click the date field and a calendar will appear. Click the correct date.

---

**🏷️ Category**
Choose one category from the dropdown. This determines which section of the blog the post appears in.

The four categories are:

- **Impact Stories** — stories about specific people and communities you have helped
- **Ministry Updates** — news about Beacon of Blessings as an organization
- **Annual Reports** — yearly summary posts
- **Community News** — news from the communities you work with

---

**⏱️ Read Time**
An estimate of how long it takes to read the post. Type this as free text.

Examples: `3 min read`, `5 min read`, `7 min read`

A rough guide: 1 minute of reading = about 200 words. Count your words and divide by 200.

---

**🏷️ Tags**
Keywords that describe the post. Tags help visitors find related content.

To add a tag:

1. Click **Add item** in the Tags section.
2. Type the tag word.
3. Add more by clicking **Add item** again.

Examples: `education`, `impact`, `Lagos`, `school supplies`, `community`, `2024`

---

**🖼️ Featured Image**
A photo that represents the post. It appears at the top of the post and as a thumbnail on the blog listing page. Follow the same upload steps as Section 4.6.

---

### 5.3 — Publish the blog post

Click the green **Publish** button at the top right. The post will appear on the blog within 60 seconds.

## 6. Managing the Gallery (Photo Albums)

The Gallery is one of the most important parts of the website — it shows the real faces and moments of your work.

### How the Gallery works

Each photo you upload in Sanity Studio becomes a **Gallery Item**. On the website, photos are displayed grouped by category (Education, Community, Events, Our Team, Impact) and sorted by date, with the most recent photos appearing first.

Think of it this way:

- **You add photos** → they appear in the Gallery on the website
- Each photo belongs to one **category** → visitors can filter by category
- The **date** you set → controls where the photo appears in the timeline

### 6.1 — Upload a new photo

Follow these steps exactly:

**Step 1 — Go to Gallery Items**
In the left sidebar, click **Gallery Item**.

**Step 2 — Create a new entry**
Click **Create new** (or the pencil icon).

**Step 3 — Fill in the Title** _(required)_
Describe what is in the photo. Be specific — this helps with searching and accessibility.

Good titles:

- `Students receiving school bags at Lagos Community School`
- `Team planning session for Digital Learning Initiative`
- `Community elders meeting with Beacon of Blessings team, August 2024`

Vague titles to avoid:

- `Photo 1`
- `IMG_4532`
- `Event`

**Step 4 — Fill in the Description** _(optional)_
A longer caption if you want to add context. This might appear as a caption beneath the photo.

Example:

> Mrs. Adebayo's class at Lagos Community School receiving their new notebooks and textbooks. September 2024.

**Step 5 — Choose a Category**
Click the Category dropdown and select one:

| Category      | Use it for                                                                 |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Education** | Classroom activities, school supply distributions, students learning       |
| **Community** | Meetings with community leaders, neighbourhood events, parent associations |
| **Events**    | Distribution events, ceremonies, formal gatherings                         |
| **Our Team**  | Photos of the Beacon of Blessings team and volunteers                      |
| **Impact**    | Before/after photos, testimonials, beneficiary stories                     |

**Step 6 — Set the Date**
Click the Date field and select when the photo was taken. This controls the order photos appear in.

**Step 7 — Upload the Image** _(required)_

1. Click **Upload** in the Image field.
2. A file picker opens. Navigate to the photo on your computer.
3. Select the photo and wait for it to upload.
4. You will see a thumbnail. If the hotspot tool appears, drag the circle to the main subject (a face or the key moment in the photo).

**Step 8 — Publish**
Click the green **Publish** button. The photo will appear in the Gallery within 60 seconds.

### 6.2 — Tips for good gallery photos

- **Well-lit** — photos taken outdoors in daylight or in well-lit rooms look best
- **Show faces** — photos with people's faces create emotional connection
- **Get consent** — always get permission before posting photos of adults, and make sure you have appropriate consent for photos of children
- **Show the impact** — a child holding a new school bag, a classroom full of engaged students, a community meeting in progress
- **Landscape orientation** (wider than tall) works best for the gallery layout

### 6.3 — Image requirements

- **Minimum width:** 1,200 pixels wide (larger is fine)
- **Accepted formats:** JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg) or PNG (.png)
- **Maximum file size:** 5 MB

If your photo is a very large file (e.g., straight from a professional camera), see Section 12 for how to resize it before uploading.

## 12. Tips and Best Practices

### Write descriptive titles

Good titles help visitors and search engines understand your content. Instead of:

- ❌ `Project Update`
- ✅ `Digital Learning Initiative — Bringing Tablets to Lagos Schools`

Instead of:

- ❌ `New Post`
- ✅ `How Our School Supplies Drive Changed Attendance Rates in Ikorodu`

### Always click Publish

This sounds obvious, but it is easy to forget. After editing anything, look for the green **Publish** button at the top right of the screen. If the button says **Publish** (not "Published"), your changes are still a draft and are not live yet.

### Use the version history if you make a mistake

Sanity keeps a full history of every published version of every document. To go back to a previous version:

1. Open the document you want to restore.
2. Look for the **clock icon** (usually on the right side of the screen) or a **History** tab.
3. Click it to see a timeline of all previous versions with dates.
4. Click on the version you want to restore.
5. Click **Restore** or **Publish** to make it the live version again.

This means you can never permanently ruin anything. You can always go back.

### Resize large photos before uploading

Photos straight from a modern smartphone or camera can be 10–20 MB. This is too large. Before uploading, resize photos to under 5 MB.

**Free tools to resize photos:**

- **On a Mac:** Open the photo in Preview → Tools menu → Adjust Size → set width to 1,600 pixels → save as JPEG
- **On Windows:** Open the photo in Paint → Image menu → Resize → set horizontal size to 1,600 pixels → save
- **Online (any device):** Go to [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) in your browser, drag your photo in, reduce quality to 80%, download

### Keep a consistent style

When writing descriptions and blog posts:

- Use complete sentences
- Capitalize proper names (Lagos, CAC, Beacon of Blessings)
- Spell out numbers below ten (one, two, three) and use numerals for 10 and above
- Use the active voice: "We distributed 500 school bags" rather than "500 school bags were distributed by us"

### Check your changes on the live website

After publishing, wait 60 seconds, then open the website in a new browser tab (or press Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows / Cmd+Shift+R on Mac to do a hard refresh) and confirm your change looks right.

## 13. Troubleshooting

### "My change is not showing on the website"

**Wait and refresh.** The website uses a caching system to stay fast. After you click Publish, changes take:

- Up to **60 seconds** for Projects, Blog Posts, and Gallery items
- Up to **5 minutes** for Founder profiles, Core Values, Impact Statistics, and Site Configuration

After waiting, do a **hard refresh**: press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac). This forces your browser to load a fresh version of the page.

If the change still does not appear after 5 minutes, contact Lionel.

---

### "I made a mistake and want to undo"

Sanity keeps the full history of every document. You can restore any previous version:

1. Open the document in Studio.
2. Look for the **clock icon** or **History** link on the right side of the screen.
3. Browse the history, find the version before your mistake, and click **Restore**.

You can also simply edit the document again to correct the mistake and click Publish again.

---

### "I accidentally deleted something"

Documents deleted in Sanity can sometimes be recovered. Contact Lionel as soon as possible. Sanity's internal history may allow recovery of deleted documents.

---

### "I can't log in to Sanity Studio"

Try these steps in order:

1. Make sure you are going to the correct Studio URL (ask Lionel if unsure).
2. Try signing in with a different browser or clear your browser cache.
3. If you have never logged in before, check your email for an invitation from Sanity. The invitation link expires after 7 days.
4. Contact Lionel at [info@beaconofblessings.org](mailto:info@beaconofblessings.org) — he can resend the invitation or check your account access.

---

### "The image I uploaded is not displaying on the website"

1. Check that you clicked **Publish** after adding the image. A draft image will not appear on the website.
2. Wait 60 seconds and do a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R).
3. Check that the image file was not corrupted — try re-uploading it.
4. If the image was larger than 5 MB, Sanity may have had trouble processing it. Resize the image and upload it again.

---

### "The image file is too large to upload"

If you see an error when uploading a photo, the file is probably too large. Resize it first:

- Go to [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) in your browser.
- Drag your photo into the page.
- On the right side, set the quality to 80% and make sure the width is no more than 2,000 pixels.
- Click **Download**.
- Upload the downloaded file to Sanity instead.

---

### "I see the old content even though I published new content"

This is almost always a browser caching issue. Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) to do a hard refresh. If you are on mobile, close and reopen the browser app.

If the issue persists after 5 minutes and a hard refresh, contact Lionel.

---

### "The slug field already has a value and I am not sure if I should change it"

**Do not change the slug of a published document.** The slug is the address of the page. Changing it breaks any links people have saved and may cause errors. If you genuinely need to change a project or post's URL, ask Lionel first so he can handle it safely.
